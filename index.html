FAQs

Quick answers about the study, the neighborhood, and how to join us.

When do you meet?

On the first Sunday of every month at 4 PM PT, unless the Partiful invite lists a one-off change. Always double-check the invite before you head out.

Where is it — Green Lake or The Little Red Hen?

We’re rooted in the Green Lake neighborhood and often tie the study to time at the park and community at The Little Red Hen. Exact meet-up details (including pin and any “start outside, then head over” flow) are posted on Partiful.

How do I RSVP?

Use our Partiful event. It helps hosts plan space, share updates, and welcome you well.

Do I have to dance, or go to a particular church?

No. Christian Dance Community welcomes friends from many backgrounds. You don’t have to identify as a dancer, and you don’t have to belong to any single church—see our participating churches for partners who help cheer this on.

What should I bring?

Your Bible (app or print is fine), curiosity, and an openness to conversation. If we’re outside, a layer for Seattle weather and something to sit on can help.

I want a Christian Dance Community T-shirt

Shirts feature Christian Dance Community on the front (left chest) and “He Leads, I Follow” on the back, in Comfort Colors fabrics. Submit the Christian Dance Community shirt order form with your color, size, lettering, delivery, and payment preferences. Orders are grouped about every other month — DM @ChristianDanceCommunity on Instagram or email ChristianDanceCommunity@gmail.com with questions (payment, pickup, shipping, etc.).

Can I invite a friend?

Yes—please do. Point them to this site and the Partiful listing so they know when and where we’re meeting.
